The normalizer renames/retags audio and subtitle tracks to one consistent scheme — it never removes tracks (that's Rules) and, for now, only edits .mkv files, using mkvpropedit to rewrite the container's metadata in place. That's why it's near-instant, unlike Rules' remux — it never touches the actual audio/video/subtitle data.
